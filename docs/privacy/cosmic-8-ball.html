🎱 About This App

Cosmic 8 Ball is an entertainment app that generates astrology-inspired answers to your questions using your zodiac sign and real-time celestial conditions — live planetary positions, moon phase, retrograde detection, and the ancient planetary hour system.

Important disclaimer: Cosmic 8 Ball is designed for entertainment, reflection, and astrology-inspired insight only. Responses are generated by a procedural celestial computation engine and should not replace professional medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice. Always trust your own judgment when making important decisions.

💾 What We Store Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally using device storage:

  • Your first name and last initial
  • Your birth date and, when provided, birth time
  • Your birth city, state/province, and country
  • Coordinates and time zone derived from the location you select, where available
  • Your calculated astrology profile, including your zodiac sun sign
  • Your question and answer history (last 100 readings)
  • Your selected mode preference (Cosmic Guidance or Party Mode)
  • Your daily cosmic pull result (refreshed each day)
  • Your audio and voice settings
  • Your notification, onboarding, display, and privacy preferences, including analytics opt-out status

Profile details, settings, and history remain on the device until you change or clear them, clear the app's data through the operating system, or uninstall the app. You can clear the saved reading history from the History screen. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app's locally stored data. There is no Cosmic 8 Ball account to delete.

The locally stored question and answer history is separate from the limited reading information transmitted through the Oracle Learning Program described below. Your zodiac sign is calculated locally but is included in that analytics payload when analytics is enabled. Your other astrology profile fields are not included in the analytics payload.

🎂 Astrology Profile Information

During setup, Cosmic 8 Ball asks for a birth date, a birth time or an indication that the time is unknown, and a birth location. Depending on the location, the saved information can include city, state/province, country, coordinates, and time zone. The app also stores the first name and last initial you enter. This information is used to:

  • Determine your zodiac sun sign
  • Personalize cosmic guidance to your astrological profile
  • Enable birthday recognition and birthday-related features

Birth location is selected from location data bundled with the app; Cosmic 8 Ball does not request your device's current GPS location for profile setup. Your first name, last initial, birth date, birth time, birth location fields, coordinates, and time zone are stored locally and are not included in the analytics payload. The zodiac sign calculated from your profile is included when analytics is enabled. Profile information is not sold or used for advertising targeting.

📊 Analytics & Oracle Learning Program

To improve the quality of cosmic guidance over time, Cosmic 8 Ball includes a reading and usage analytics feature called the Oracle Learning Program. Analytics is enabled by default. You can disable it at any time in Settings. When enabled, the following information is transmitted after a completed reading:

  • Timestamp
  • Mode (Cosmic Guidance or Party Mode)
  • Your zodiac sign
  • Question topic/category
  • The user-entered question
  • The generated answer
  • Answer strength
  • App version
🔒 Privacy Reminder Free-text questions can contain information that you choose to enter. Please do not include names, contact details, medical information, financial information, or other sensitive personal information. Before transmission, the app attempts to replace email addresses, common U.S. phone-number formats, Social Security numbers written with spaces or hyphens, and grouped 16-digit card numbers. This limited pattern-based redaction may not recognize every identifier, format, name, account number, or sensitive detail.

Where Analytics Data Is Stored

The payload is sent via HTTPS to a Google Apps Script web endpoint and stored in an access-controlled Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the developer. Google provides the Apps Script and Sheets infrastructure and may process the transmission and storage under Google's applicable service terms. The developer does not sell this information or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

Purpose

Analytics data is used to improve the Pantheon Engine's answer quality, variety, topic coverage, and overall user experience. It is not used for advertising targeting or cross-app tracking.

Opting Out

You can disable analytics at any time inside the app: Settings → Oracle Learning Program → toggle off. When disabled, new completed readings are not transmitted by the Oracle Learning Program. Your opt-out preference is stored locally on your device and persists across app sessions.

No Persistent User Identifier in the Payload The analytics payload does not intentionally include an account identifier, advertising identifier, device identifier, first name, last initial, birth date, birth time, birth location, coordinates, time zone, reading-history file, voice setting, or audio preference. However, question text may contain information entered by the user, and the payload includes the calculated zodiac sign.

Analytics Retention and Deletion

The current analytics system does not enforce a fixed automatic expiration period. Analytics rows remain in the private spreadsheet until they are manually removed by the developer.

You may send an analytics deletion request to support@populationplusone.com with “Cosmic 8 Ball analytics deletion request” in the subject line. The analytics payload has no account or persistent device identifier, so the developer cannot automatically identify which rows belong to you. Include enough information to search for matching rows, such as the approximate date and time, question text, mode, and zodiac sign. Matching rows can be deleted when they can be reliably identified; if the supplied information is insufficient, it may not be possible to locate the rows.

🌌 How Responses Are Generated

Responses in Cosmic 8 Ball are produced by a procedural celestial computation engine running entirely on your device. The engine uses Meeus orbital algorithms to calculate real-time positions for all 8 planets, detects moon phase and retrograde status, applies the ancient Chaldean planetary hour system, and scores your sun sign against current sky conditions to produce a dynamic cosmic alignment score.

All calculations run locally within the app. No AI service or language model is used. The question you type is used to classify the topic and generate your personalized reading, and it is included in the Oracle Learning Program payload when analytics is enabled.

🎙️ Microphone & Speech Recognition

Cosmic 8 Ball includes an optional voice input feature. If you choose to use it:

  • The app requests permission to access your microphone
  • Speech recognition is handled by the speech-recognition service configured on your device, such as Apple's or Google's service
  • Audio is not recorded or stored by the app
  • Depending on the device, operating system, selected recognition service, and settings, speech audio may be processed on the device or sent to that provider for processing under its privacy terms
  • The resulting transcript becomes the question in the app and can be included in the Oracle Learning Program payload when analytics is enabled

Microphone access is entirely optional. You can type your questions instead and decline microphone permission without affecting any other app feature.

🔊 Text-to-Speech

If voice output is enabled, Cosmic 8 Ball sends generated answer text to the text-to-speech engine configured on your device so the answer can be spoken aloud. The app does not record the spoken output. Text-to-speech engines differ: some synthesize entirely on the device, while others may use a provider's network service depending on the installed engine, voice, operating-system settings, and provider terms. Cosmic 8 Ball does not control those provider-specific processing choices. You can disable voice output in the app's settings.

🔔 Notifications

Cosmic 8 Ball may request permission to send local notifications (e.g., a daily cosmic pull reminder). If you grant permission:

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device
  • No notification content is sent through external servers
  • You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app

Notifications are optional. The app is fully functional without them.

📡 Sensors & Device Data

The app uses your device's motion sensors (accelerometer) to detect shake gestures for submitting questions. Sensor data is used only in real time and is never stored or transmitted.

🌐 Network & Third Parties

Cosmic 8 Ball can use the Google Apps Script and Google Sheets analytics infrastructure described above. Optional speech recognition and text-to-speech may also use the provider configured on your device. No advertising SDK, Firebase Analytics, Sentry, account/login service, or payment service is used by the current app.

The app contains cross-promotion links to Written in the Stars (our companion horoscope app). Tapping these links opens your device's browser or the app store — no data is sent from the app itself when you tap these links.

There are no advertising networks, social media SDKs, or third-party data brokers integrated into the app.

🚫 Information the App Does Not Request

Cosmic 8 Ball does not request the following as profile or account fields:

  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Current GPS location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)
  • Payment information
  • Device identifiers
  • Account or login credentials

If you voluntarily include personal or sensitive information in a typed or dictated question, it can be included in analytics when the Oracle Learning Program is enabled. The limited redaction described above should not be treated as a guarantee that all such information will be removed.
