🎱 About This App

Cosmic 8 Ball is an entertainment app that generates astrology-inspired answers to your questions using your zodiac sign and real-time cosmic conditions (moon phase, planetary positions, time of day).

Important disclaimer: Cosmic 8 Ball is designed for entertainment, reflection, and astrology-inspired insight only. Responses are generated by AI and should not replace professional medical, legal, financial, or mental health advice. Always trust your own judgment when making important decisions.

💾 What We Store (Locally on Your Device)

Cosmic 8 Ball stores the following data locally using your device's AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your first name (entered during onboarding)
  • Your birth month, day, and year
  • Your calculated zodiac sun sign
  • Your question and answer history (readings log)
  • Your selected tone mode preference (Cosmic, Mixed, Fun, Party)
  • Your daily cosmic pull result (refreshed each day)

You can delete all stored data by uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete and no server-side data to request removal of.

🤖 AI-Generated Content

Responses in Cosmic 8 Ball are generated using on-device logic combining astrology-inspired algorithms with real-time cosmic inputs. Your questions and the generated responses are processed entirely on your device.

No questions you ask are transmitted to any external AI service, server, or third party. The AI logic runs locally within the app.

🎙️ Microphone & Speech Recognition

Cosmic 8 Ball includes an optional voice input feature. If you choose to use it:

  • The app requests permission to access your microphone
  • Speech recognition is handled by the device's built-in speech recognition system (Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer on iOS, Google Speech on Android)
  • Audio is not recorded or stored by the app
  • Speech data may be processed by Apple or Google per their respective privacy policies

Microphone access is entirely optional. You can type your questions instead and decline microphone permission without affecting any other app feature.

🔔 Notifications

Cosmic 8 Ball may request permission to send local notifications (e.g., a daily cosmic pull reminder). If you grant permission:

  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device
  • No notification content is sent through external servers
  • You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app

Notifications are optional. The app is fully functional without them.

📡 Sensors & Device Data

The app uses your device's motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope) to create immersive visual effects — for example, the eyeball may respond to how you hold your device. Sensor data is used only in real time for visual rendering and is never stored or transmitted.

🌐 Network & Third Parties

Cosmic 8 Ball does not make network requests to any external server for its core functionality. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services embedded in the app.

The only external link in the app is a cross-promotion banner in the "How It Works" screen linking to Written in the Stars on the Play Store. Tapping this link opens your device's browser — no data is sent from the app itself.

👶 Children's Privacy

Cosmic 8 Ball is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it.
