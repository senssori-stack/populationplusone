🎡 About This App

SuperFly Sky Wheels is an interactive spinning wheel experience that responds to your device's motion, tilt, and surrounding environment to create dynamic visual effects. It is a pure entertainment experience with no user accounts, no social features, and no data collection.

💾 Data We Collect

We collect no personal information.

SuperFly Sky Wheels does not ask for your name, email, date of birth, or any identifying information. The only data stored on your device is your app preferences (e.g., selected wheel mode, last used settings), which are stored locally and never transmitted.

📡 Motion Sensors

SuperFly Sky Wheels uses your device's built-in motion sensors — including the accelerometer, gyroscope, and barometer — to create responsive visual effects:

  • Wheel spin speed responds to how you move and tilt your device
  • Visual effects adapt to detected motion intensity
  • Atmospheric overlays may change based on barometric pressure readings

Sensor data is read in real time and used only for immediate visual rendering. It is never stored, logged, or transmitted. No sensor data ever leaves your device.

📍 Location

SuperFly Sky Wheels may request approximate location access to personalize atmospheric visual effects — for example, adapting sky colors to your local time of day or weather region.

  • Only approximate (city-level) location is used
  • Location is read at the moment you open the relevant feature
  • Location data is not stored or transmitted anywhere
  • The app functions fully without location access

You can deny location permission and the app will use default visual settings instead.

🌐 Network & Third Parties

SuperFly Sky Wheels makes no network requests. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, social login SDKs, or crash reporting services embedded in the app. The app operates entirely offline.

👶 Children's Privacy

SuperFly Sky Wheels collects no personal information from any user, including children. The app is suitable for all ages. No data is collected, stored, or shared from any user regardless of age.
