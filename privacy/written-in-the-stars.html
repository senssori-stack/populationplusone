✨ About This App

Written in the Stars provides daily horoscopes, birth chart calculations, planetary transit readings, and compatibility analysis personalized to your exact birth date, time, and location.

All astrological calculations are performed on your device using the birth data you provide. Your birth information never leaves your device.

💾 What We Store (Locally on Your Device)

Written in the Stars stores the following data locally using your device's storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Your birth date, time, and place of birth
  • Your calculated natal chart (sun, moon, rising, and all planetary positions)
  • Saved compatibility profiles (names and birth data of people you've added)
  • Your daily horoscope reading history
  • Your saved transit alerts and notification preferences
  • App settings and preferences

You can delete all stored data by uninstalling the app. Individual profiles can be deleted from within the app.

📍 Location

Written in the Stars may request access to your location to:

  • Determine your precise birth location for accurate rising sign and house calculations
  • Apply local time zone corrections to planetary positions
  • Calculate current sky conditions relative to your location

Location data is used only at the time of the request to perform astrological calculations. It is not stored persistently, not shared with any third party, and not transmitted off your device. Location access is optional — you can enter your birth location manually.

👥 Contacts

Written in the Stars may request access to your contacts to make it easier to add compatibility profiles for people you know. If you grant access:

  • You choose which contacts to import — the app does not bulk-import your contact list
  • Only the name you select is used; phone numbers, emails, and other contact details are not accessed
  • Contact data is never transmitted off your device

Contacts access is entirely optional. You can enter names manually without granting this permission.

🔔 Notifications

Written in the Stars may request permission to send local notifications for:

  • Daily horoscope reminders
  • Significant planetary transit alerts (e.g., Mercury retrograde)
  • New moon and full moon reminders

All notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content passes through an external server. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

📡 Sensors

The app uses your device's motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer) to create immersive visual effects such as star field parallax and constellation animations. Sensor data is used only in real time for visual rendering and is never stored or transmitted.

🌐 Network & Third Parties

Written in the Stars does not transmit personal data to any external server. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporting services embedded in the app.

All astrological calculations — including planetary positions, house cusps, aspects, and transit data — are computed on-device using astronomical algorithms.

👶 Children's Privacy

Written in the Stars is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will delete it.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date above and may notify you through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
