👶 About This App

Population +1 is a keepsake companion for new parents. It helps you track your baby's milestones, save precious memories, record firsts, play lullabies, and celebrate your newborn's arrival — all in a private, family-only space.

We take the privacy of your family's data — especially data about infants and young children — with the utmost seriousness. The entire app is designed to work without any internet connection or user account.

💾 What We Store (Locally on Your Device)

Population +1 stores the following data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server:

  • Your baby's name, date of birth, and birth details (weight, length, time)
  • Milestone records and dates (first smile, first steps, first words, etc.)
  • Notes and memory entries you create
  • Keepsake images you choose to save within the app
  • Lullaby playback preferences
  • App settings and preferences

You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app. All data is stored using your device's secure local storage.

🖼️ Photos & Media Library

Population +1 may request access to your photo library to let you add keepsake photos to your baby's memory book. If you grant access:

  • You choose which photos to add — the app never bulk-accesses your library
  • Selected images are saved within the app's local storage on your device
  • Photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any third party
  • The app can also save milestone illustrations to your photo library if you choose to export them

Photo library access is optional. The app is fully usable without it.

👥 Contacts

Population +1 may request access to your contacts to make it easier to share birth announcements or add family members to your keepsake book. If you grant access:

  • You choose which contacts to reference — the app does not import your entire contact list
  • Contact information is used only to pre-fill fields you choose; it is not stored by the app
  • No contact data is transmitted off your device

Contacts access is optional. You can enter names and details manually.

🎵 Lullabies & Audio

Population +1 includes a built-in lullaby player. Audio files are bundled within the app itself — no audio is streamed from the internet. The app may request background audio permission so lullabies continue playing while the screen is off or while you use other apps. No audio data is recorded or transmitted.

📡 Sensors

The app may use your device's motion sensors for interactive animations and gentle shake-to-play features. Sensor data is used only in real time for interactive effects and is never stored or transmitted.

📍 Location

Population +1 may request location access to record your baby's birth location as part of the keepsake birth announcement. Location is used only if you explicitly add it to a memory entry. It is stored locally in that memory entry and is never transmitted. Location access is optional.

🌐 Network & Third Parties

Population +1 makes no network requests. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or data brokers embedded in the app. The app is designed to work entirely offline.

We do not use crash reporting services that might capture device information or app state.

🧒 Children's Data

Population +1 is designed to store information about infants and young children, entered by parents. We take this responsibility seriously:

  • All data about children is stored exclusively on the parent's device
  • No child data is transmitted to any server, third party, or cloud service
  • We do not build profiles on children for any purpose
  • The app is intended for use by adults (parents/guardians) on their own devices

Population +1 complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) by design — we do not collect any online information from or about children.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date above and may notify you through the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
