Our Story

Built with Intention

Population Plus One is an independent mobile app studio focused on experiences that feel personal, private, and genuinely useful.

Population Plus One started with a simple frustration: most apps that handle your most personal moments — your baby's birth, your birth chart, your deepest questions — are wrapped in advertising networks, cloud sync requirements, and data collection you never asked for.

We build differently. Every app in the Population Plus One ecosystem is designed around one non-negotiable: your data belongs to you. Not to an ad network. Not to a cloud platform. Not to us.

That constraint — privacy first, always — turns out to be a surprisingly powerful design principle. When you don't need a server, you build for offline. When you don't need an account, you build for simplicity. When you don't need to monetize data, you build apps people actually trust.

The result is a growing suite of apps that each tackle a different meaningful moment — welcoming a new life, exploring your place in the cosmos, chasing the sky, seeking answers in the stars. Four apps. One philosophy.

What We Believe

Our Values

🔒
Privacy Is Non-Negotiable
We don't run ad networks. We don't sell data. We don't require accounts. Your personal information stays on your device, always.
🌐
Offline First
Your experience shouldn't depend on a server, a network, or a subscription. Most features work completely offline.
✦
Craft Over Quantity
Every animation, every screen, every interaction is considered. We'd rather build four great apps than forty mediocre ones.
🤖
AI With Transparency
When AI generates content in our apps, we say so clearly. Entertainment is entertainment. Advice is advice. We never blur the line.
👶
Family-Grade Privacy
Apps that handle children's data are held to an even higher standard. COPPA compliance isn't a checkbox — it's a design principle.
🚀
Ship and Improve
We're a small team that moves fast and listens closely. Every update reflects real feedback from real users.
